About ReelPath

A small, honest "before you press play" tool — not a studio, not an agency, not a data operation.

What this is

ReelPath is a personal, zero-cost side project built by Shahedi Bilash. It exists to answer two questions cleanly: "what order do I watch this franchise in?" and "what's actually good in this genre?" — without an account, a paywall, or fifteen minutes of scrolling past ads to get an answer.

It's built the same way as Bilash's other project, The Bangladesh Trip — static pages, no backend, hosted free on Cloudflare Pages, and growing through search and word of mouth rather than ad spend.

Credits & sources

Watch-order data (episode ranges, arc names, film release dates) is compiled from publicly available franchise information and cross-checked against multiple fan and official sources. Episode numbers in particular can drift slightly between regions and streaming platforms — always sanity-check the first and last episode of an arc against what you're actually watching.

Genre picks reflect editorial judgment, not an algorithm or a sponsor. Nothing on this site is paid placement.

Poster art and stills, once added, will come from The Movie Database (TMDB). This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Affiliate disclosure

Some "where to watch" links on this site are, or will become, affiliate links — ReelPath may earn a small commission if you use them, at no extra cost to you. That never affects which titles get recommended or how they're ranked.

Something wrong, or missing?

Spot an incorrect episode number, a franchise you'd like a guide for, or a genre pick you strongly disagree with? Email Shahedi directly — this is a one-person project and every message actually gets read.
