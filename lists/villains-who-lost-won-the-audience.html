Character list

Villains who lost but won the audience

Every one of these lost the actual fight. None of them lost the room.

8.28.401
ThanosAvengers: Endgame · 2019

Lost the final battle, but his "the universe needed balancing" logic still sparks the most debate of any MCU villain.

7.47.302
KillmongerBlack Panther · 2018

Died on Wakandan soil, but his critique of the country's isolationism reshaped the entire sequel's theme.

8.68.903
Light YagamiDeath Note · 2006

Caught and killed, but "was he actually wrong?" is still a live argument two decades later.

6.77.104
General ZodMan of Steel · 2013

Snapped his neck and lost everything, yet "I exist only to protect Krypton" remains genuinely sympathetic.

8.18.005
LokiThe Avengers · 2012

Defeated in the first team-up, then somehow became beloved enough to headline his own show.

7.07.406
MagnetoX2: X-Men United · 2003

Outmaneuvered every time, but his argument for mutant self-defense never stops landing.

7.57.807
Baron ZemoCaptain America: Civil War · 2016

Ends the film in a cell, having broken the Avengers apart with nothing but patience and grief.

8.508
Cersei LannisterGame of Thrones · 2011

Buried under the Red Keep, still one of the most quoted, most complicated villains TV has produced.

8.28.309
AmonThe Legend of Korra · 2012

Exposed as a fraud and defeated, but his revolutionary rhetoric about bender privilege still resonates.

8.18.110
Bellatrix LestrangeHarry Potter and the Deathly Hallows: Part 2 · 2011

Killed by Molly Weasley in one line, and still the most magnetic performance in the franchise's back half.
