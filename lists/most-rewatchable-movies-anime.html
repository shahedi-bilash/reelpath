Comfort-watch list

Most rewatchable movies & anime

The ones you reach for on a bad day without even thinking about it — not necessarily the “best,” just the ones that never wear out.

8.58.601
Spirited Away2001

A world this detailed rewards rewatching — there's always a background detail or a quiet emotional beat you missed the first time.

7.57.502
John Wick2014

Grief turned into the cleanest gunfight choreography in modern action — just as satisfying on the tenth watch.

8.18.003
The Avengers2012

The blueprint for the shared-universe crossover, still fun purely as a hangout movie with occasional punching.

8.08.204
Kill Bill: Vol. 12003

Pure genre pastiche swung like a katana — built for rewatching scene by scene.

8.58.905
Cowboy BebopAnime · 1998

Episodic by design, so you can drop in anywhere and still get the show's whole mood in twenty minutes.

8.79.106
Fullmetal Alchemist: BrotherhoodAnime · 2009

A complete, well-paced story with a payoff so tight it's worth rewatching just to catch the setup again.

8.08.107
The Grand Budapest Hotel2014

So densely composed that every rewatch turns up another visual joke buried in the frame.

8.79.108
Attack on TitanAnime · 2013

The foreshadowing is dense enough that a second watch plays almost like a different show.

8.68.509
Jujutsu KaisenAnime · 2020

Fight choreography built for freeze-frame rewatching, with stakes that hit harder once you know what's coming.

8.08.310
Toy Story1995
