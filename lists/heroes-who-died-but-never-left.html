Heroes who died but never left

Their screen time ended. Their influence on everyone else didn't.

8.201
Tony StarkAvengers: Endgame · 2019

Died on-screen snapping his fingers, and "I am Iron Man" is still the line the whole saga gets measured against.

8.802
Portgas D. AceOne Piece · 1999

His death at Marineford reshaped Luffy's entire character for the hundreds of episodes that followed.

03
JiraiyaNaruto Shippuden · 2007

Died mid-series, but his lessons keep echoing in Naruto's choices all the way to the finale.

8.204
Obi-Wan KenobiStar Wars · 1977

Struck down by Vader, then spends the rest of the original trilogy guiding Luke as a voice from beyond.

8.405
BoromirThe Lord of the Rings: The Fellowship of the Ring · 2001

Dies defending Merry and Pippin, and that redemption echoes through his brother and father's arcs for two more films.

8.306
MufasaThe Lion King · 1994

Dies in the first act, but "remember who you are" carries Simba through the rest of the film.

8.507
Ned StarkGame of Thrones · 2011

Executed at the end of season one, and nearly every character's motivation traces back to his memory.

08
Itachi UchihaNaruto Shippuden · 2007

Died years before the story even starts, and the reveal of why he did what he did redefines the entire Uchiha arc.

7.209
RueThe Hunger Games · 2012

Dies early in the Games, but her flower salute becomes the trilogy's symbol of quiet rebellion.

7.710
Sirius BlackHarry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix · 2007

Falls through a veil in one scene, but his belief in Harry shapes every choice Harry makes afterward.
