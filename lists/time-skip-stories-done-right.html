Anime structure list

Time-skip stories done right

The jump-forward move every long-running series eventually makes — these are the ones that used it instead of just surviving it.

8.801
One Piece1999

The two-year post-Marineford skip is the gold standard — everyone comes back changed, not just stronger.

8.402
Naruto → Naruto Shippuden2002 / 2007

The skip that redefined the series, aging up the entire cast and resetting the stakes.

8.703
Attack on Titan2013

A four-year jump forward that quietly recontextualizes who the good guys even are.

7.904
Fairy Tail2009

A seven-year freeze for the whole guild, so everyone returns to a world that moved on without them.

8.405
Dragon Ball Z1989

The three-year training gap before the Android arc is the timeskip every shonen since has copied.

8.506
Vinland Saga2019

The jump from raider-era violence to a quiet farm changes the entire tone of the story on purpose.

8.407
Bleach2004

A seventeen-month gap used for a full cast power-up reveal, all at once.

7.808
Boruto: Naruto Next Generations2017

A generational leap forward, handing the story to Naruto's kids instead of aging the original cast further.

8.609
JoJo's Bizarre Adventure2012

Built entirely around generational skips — decades between each new Joestar's story.

8.710
Hunter x Hunter2011

The story doesn't just time-skip on screen — its real-world hiatuses might be the biggest time skip of all.
