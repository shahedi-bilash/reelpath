Best plot twists

Spoiler territory, by design — these are the reveals that made rewatching the whole thing mandatory.

8.001
The Sixth Sense1999

Malcolm Crowe has been dead the entire film — a reveal that rewrites every scene that came before it.

8.502
Parasite2019

The basement reveal doesn't just twist the plot, it flips the film's entire genre halfway through.

8.703
Eren's true roleAttack on Titan · 2013

Eren's true role in the Titans' history unfolds gradually, and it recontextualizes everything before it.

8.404
Fight Club1999

Tyler Durden was never a separate person — one of the most imitated twists in modern film.

8.205
The Usual Suspects1995

Verbal Kint's entire testimony unravels in the film's last two minutes.

8.206
Oldboy2003

The truth about Mi-do is the kind of twist that makes you want to un-know it.

7.907
Gone Girl2014

Amy staged her entire disappearance — and the film keeps twisting well past that reveal.

8.208
The Prestige2006

Two magicians, one impossible trick, and a twin secret hiding in plain sight the whole time.

8.209
Shutter Island2010

Teddy Daniels isn't investigating a case — he's the patient the entire asylum is treating.

8.410
Psycho1960

Norman Bates and his "mother" are the same person — the reveal that basically invented the modern twist ending.
