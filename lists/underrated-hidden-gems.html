How this list gets made

Unlike the rest of the site's picks, this one starts from data, not a hunch: TMDB titles with a vote average of 7.5+ but a comparatively low vote count — the combination that tends to flag films critics and cinephiles love but general audiences never really found. The raw candidate list still gets sanity-checked by hand before anything lands here, so nothing appears without an accurate, confident description.
