What's different about Lists

Watch-order guides tell you what sequence to press play in. Best-of pages rank whole titles by genre. Lists are neither — they're the character- and moment-level opinions that don't fit either format: a villain worth rooting for, a scene that outlived its film, a death that reshaped a story for hundreds of episodes after.

Got a list you'd want to see? Send a request — new ones get added over time.
