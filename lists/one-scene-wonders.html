One-scene wonders

Brief screen time, outsized impact — these moments did more in minutes than most films do in two hours.

01
Heath Ledger's JokerThe Dark Knight · 2008
8.59.1

The interrogation-room scene alone defined the character for a generation.

02
Alfred Molina's Doc OckSpider-Man: No Way Home · 2021
7.98.1

A few minutes of screen time became the film's single biggest fan-reaction moment.

03
Matthew McConaugheyThe Wolf of Wall Street · 2013
8.08.2

One chest-thumping lunch scene, and it's the moment most people remember first.

04
Alec BaldwinGlengarry Glen Ross · 1992
7.37.6

"Always be closing" — one monologue, and it's the reason anyone still talks about this film.

05
Samuel L. JacksonDeep Blue Sea · 1999
6.05.9

A mid-speech shark attack that remains one of the great subverted-expectations moments in genre film.

06
Might GuyNaruto Shippuden · 2007
8.58.7

Opening all Eight Gates against Madara is a single sequence fans still point to as the series' emotional peak.

07
Christoph WaltzInglourious Basterds · 2009
8.28.4

The farmhouse interrogation is ten minutes long and introduces Hans Landa as one of cinema's great villains instantly.

08
Jack NicholsonA Few Good Men · 1992
7.67.7

"You can't handle the truth!" — a single courtroom outburst that outlived the rest of the film's reputation.

09
Rutger HauerBlade Runner · 1982
7.98.1

The "tears in rain" monologue, delivered in the film's final minutes, is largely his own improvisation.

10
James Earl JonesStar Wars: The Empire Strikes Back · 1980
8.48.7

"No, I am your father" — one line that rewrote the entire saga in real time.
