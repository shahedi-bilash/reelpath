Should I watch the compilation movies instead?

No — treat them as optional, not a substitute. Attack on Titan has several theatrical compilation films (Crimson Bow and Arrow, Wings of Freedom, and others) that re-cut existing TV episodes into a shorter runtime, sometimes with a few minutes of exclusive footage or altered scenes. They're a decent refresher before a new season if it's been a while, but they skip material and aren't the complete story. This guide covers the full TV series, which is the version that actually tells the whole thing.
