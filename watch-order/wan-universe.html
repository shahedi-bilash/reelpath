Which order is actually better here?

Chronological is the fun way to watch this franchise — it's a slow reveal of the Warrens' career across decades, and Annabelle's doll ends up in their case room by the "end" for a reason. Release order works fine too and is what most fans watched first, but you'll notice the timeline jumping around a lot.
