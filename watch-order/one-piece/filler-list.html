One Piece filler list

Just the filler — seven anime-only arcs, 41 episodes total, none of them required. If you want the full arc-by-arc path with posters and a live toggle, see the full watch order guide instead. This page is the quick-reference version.

✕
Warship IslandEp 54–618 episodes

An anime-only detour right before Alabasta. Not in the manga — skip freely.

✕
Goat IslandEp 131–1355 episodes

A one-off stop right after Alabasta with zero bearing on the main plot.

✕
G-8Ep 196–20611 episodes

A filler arc at a Marine base — no manga content, purely anime pacing.

✕
Foxy's ReturnEp 220–2245 episodes

A rematch with a filler-only villain from an earlier non-canon arc.

✕
Z's AmbitionEp 626–6283 episodes

A short tie-in to One Piece Film: Z — optional even for completionists.

✕
Silver MineEp 747–7504 episodes

A brief filler stop between Dressrosa and Zou.

✕
Cidre GuildEp 879–88911 episodes

Anime-only bridge material between Whole Cake Island and Wano.

That's 41 episodes total, out of 1,000+

One Piece has one of the best canon-to-filler ratios of any long-running anime — these seven arcs are essentially the entire list. Skip all of them with zero loss to the main story; nothing here pays off later. If an episode isn't listed above, it's canon.

Why is this a separate page from the main guide?

The full One Piece watch order shows these filler arcs in context, interactive and toggleable alongside the canon sagas. This page exists for the simpler question people actually search for: just the skip list, nothing else.
