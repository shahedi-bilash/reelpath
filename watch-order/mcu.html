Chronological placements follow the most commonly cited in-universe timeline — a few films (Eternals, Deadpool & Wolverine) span huge time ranges and are placed by their main present-day setting. Disney+ series aren't in this pass yet. Films released after this guide was written won't appear until we update it.

Which order should you actually pick?

First time through? Go release order — it's how the surprises (the first team-up, the Thanos reveal, the snap) were designed to land, and it matches every "MCU in order" list you'll find elsewhere. Rewatching, or want the timeline to make sense on its own terms? Chronological order removes the flash-forwards and flashbacks and just tells the story start to finish.

Do I need to watch the Disney+ shows too?

Not to follow the films — this guide is films-only for now. Shows like WandaVision, Loki and Hawkeye add context but skipping them won't leave you lost.
