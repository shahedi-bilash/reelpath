Is there really no trick to this one?

No trick. Every other franchise on this site needs a toggle or a chronology switch for a reason — a chaotic timeline, filler episodes, or a release order that scrambles the story. Harry Potter doesn't have any of that: eight films, one book each (with Deathly Hallows split into two), released in the exact order the story happens in. If you're new to watch-order guides, this is the one that shows what "no confusion" actually looks like.
