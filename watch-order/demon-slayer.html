Is there any filler to skip?

Practically none — Demon Slayer is unusually faithful to Koyoharu Gotouge's manga, and the anime almost never pads its runtime with anime-only detours. The one thing worth knowing: Mugen Train isn't a side story you can skip. It's a direct continuation of Season 1 and the story doesn't make sense without it.
